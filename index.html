Closing

Two men, two choices, two results

Adam
  1. Chose to disobey God
  2. Sin spoiled the world
  3. Death came to all people
vs
Jesus
  1. Chose to obey His Father
  2. Died on the cross for our sin
  3. Believers get righteousness and eternal life!

God is gracious. He didn’t leave us stuck in Adam’s sin. Jesus’s righteousness defeated sin!

Let’s pray: Thank you, God, for the gift of your grace. Thank you for sending Jesus to die for our sin. Thank you that your grace brings eternal life to all who trust in Jesus as Lord. Amen.
