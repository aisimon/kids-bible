Romans 5:1–11 · Kids Lesson

Friends with God again!

Have you ever had a fight with a friend, and then made up? Today we learn a big Bible word for making up.

Reconcile Say it: REK-un-syle

To reconcile means to become friends again after being apart. Romans 5 says Jesus made a way for us to be friends with God again.

Romans 5:6, 10

Uh-oh, a big gap

Sin means choosing our own way instead of God’s way. Sin made a huge gap between us and God. Can we jump across by ourselves? Tap a way to try!

“For while we were yet weak, at the right time Christ died for the ungodly.”Romans 5:6 (WEB)
Romans 5:8–10 · Memory verse

God built the bridge!

We could not cross the gap, so God came to us. Jesus died on the cross for our sins and rose again. The cross is the bridge back to God!

“But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”Romans 5:8 (WEB) · Say it 3 times together!
