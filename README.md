# kids-bible

Short, colorful, interactive kids' Bible lessons.

## Romans · Lesson 9: God's Gift of Righteousness (Romans 5:12–21)

**Main truth:** Jesus's righteousness defeated sin. **God's attribute:** God is gracious.

A 10-page interactive presentation that follows the Lesson 9 lesson guide:

| Page | Lesson part | Kids do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title & main truth | Open the gift to reveal the main truth |
| 2 | Build Community (10 min) | Inheritance game: keep the offered item or trade it for a numbered treasure box |
| 3 | Lesson Introduction | Flip the cards: what we inherit from Adam vs. through Jesus |
| 4 | True or False? (5:12–14) | Answer true/false; wrong answers show the correction |
| 5 | Adam or Jesus? (5:15–17) | Drag statements into the Adam or Jesus column (or tap, then tap) |
| 6 | Correct the Mistakes (5:18–19) | Tap the wrong word and pick the right one |
| 7 | Find the Answer (5:20–21) | Fill the blanks from a word bank |
| 8 | God is gracious + Memory Verse (5:18) | “Hide a word” until kids can say the verse by heart |
| 9 | Vocabulary + Discussion | Match trespass, judgment, reign, provision; reveal answers |
| 10 | Closing (5 min) | Two men, two choices, two results; prayer; next-week question |

Open `index.html` in any browser. It's a single file with no build step; fonts load from Google Fonts.

- Move with **Back/Next**, the dots, the arrow keys, or a swipe.
- **✎ Notes** (or `L`) shows leader notes for each page: timings, what to ask, and the leader tip.
- **♪ Music** (or `M`) plays a cheerful background tune; taps also play small sound effects. All sounds are synthesized in the browser (an original melody, no audio files).

The memory verse is quoted from the NIV, as in the lesson guide. The rest of the wording is a kid-level paraphrase of the guide.

The previous lesson (Romans 5:1–11, “Friends with God Again”) is kept on the `old` branch.
